# PRIME Student Dashboard Presentation Contract v1.1

**Classification:** CANONICAL CORE / constitutional compatibility revision
**Adoption:** Proposed; pending review and merge.
**Authority:** [System Governance Canon](governance/PRIME-SYSTEM-GOVERNANCE-CANON.md).
**Base:** [Presentation Contract v1](student-dashboard-presentation-contract-v1.md), with the explicit changes below.

## 1. Temporal structure

Keep **NOW / RECENT / MEMORY**, shared components and individualized authorized data. NOW can be incomplete. Do not infer CEFR advancement, attendance, state changes or percentages from activity counts. Preserve authorized account/learner isolation and complete published lesson history.

## 2. Continuity without a state-change requirement

What Changed remains reserved for an actual supported pedagogical change. Do not rename every maintenance or revisit event as progress merely to fill that card.

The absence of What Changed must not remove the learning story or produce a default no-progress message. RECENT presents authorized learning episodes, including familiar work returning or being used again; MEMORY preserves their sequence and cumulative significance. A separate learning-event account does not depend on a state-transition flag.

Use natural headings such as What we noticed and What comes next. Do not add a fourth temporal layer. Do not imply an uninterrupted upward trajectory or a fixed time to the target level.

## 3. Progress Tracker interpretation

The existing labels **Strong / Improving / Needs Focus / Not Assessed** and their semantic colors remain available for authorized, scoped skill summaries. This revision supersedes any interpretation of their listed order in the [Frozen Standard](PRIME_PROGRESS_TRACKER_FROZEN_STANDARD.md) as an ordinal learning ladder. No fifth state is introduced.

A label is not an assessment algorithm. Unknown raw labels must not be promoted to a positive or negative judgment. Neutral presentation must preserve independently authorized narrative evidence: **Not Assessed must not erase a learning episode.** Evidence of an event and sufficient evidence to classify a stable skill are different thresholds.

Do not infer an Improving state from the mere presence of a word such as developing. Classify through authorized evidence and contextual teacher interpretation; legacy wording normalization is not new pedagogical authority. This narrows the older normalization rule where it would strengthen a claim.

The explanation alongside a status should describe what happened, relevant conditions and a useful next opportunity. A learner must not receive a deficit classification because there was no opportunity to reuse a bounded exam-preparation topic.

## 4. Shared narrative and disclosure

Dashboard summaries and full Portfolio reports may differ in length, but must agree on the same authorized meaning. A summary must not compress attempt, cue and adjustment into an error label or lose a boundary essential to its claim.

Keep source lineage and support details in teacher/audit views; explain support naturally when it matters to the learner-facing claim. No engineering language, invented percentages or scores derived from state categories. Practice scores, if provided elsewhere, remain task-specific and do not automatically update learner state.

## 5. Acceptance

Verify at least these cases in the later implementation:

1. Same recorded state plus an authorized revisit event: the event remains visible.
2. No classified skill plus a valid narrative: neutral status does not hide the narrative.
3. Bounded school-support topic: purpose and known opportunities constrain interpretation.
4. Documented adjustment: the sequence and cue remain distinguishable from spontaneous self-correction.
5. Missing evidence: no fabricated story, achievement or deficit.
6. Teacher-authorized state change: visible within the journey and its actual scope.
7. Language-mediated content task: document what the learner accessed or participated in, with relevant support, without converting it into independent subject mastery or requiring later recall to establish all of its value.

This is a versioned contract, not a runtime change. Existing tests must be updated deliberately where they encode superseded semantics; do not remove identity, authority or historical-integrity protection.
