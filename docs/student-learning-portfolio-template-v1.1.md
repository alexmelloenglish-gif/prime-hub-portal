## Preserved design and structure

Template v1.0's nineteen-section order remains the visual and structural base. Preserve its typography, palette, reusable components, quick-access roles, attendance meanings and separation of scheduling from pedagogical next actions. This is an explicit version of semantic rules, not a second competing layout.

The Google Docs master named in v1.0 remains the existing visual reference; this amendment does not claim that the external master has been updated.

## Semantic amendments to v1.0

- **Current Learning State:** a scoped, authorized synthesis alongside the journey. A retained level is not a conclusion that learning stopped.
- **Strengths & Evidence:** describe supported events and their meaning; contextual support is not a learner identity or the only measure of development.
- **Learning Patterns:** require evidence of recurrence; separate ordinary variation and missing opportunity from a claimed pattern of difficulty.
- **Learning focus and priorities:** distinguish learning English for personal expression, using English to access another subject/task, and understanding that subject. A documented contribution in one dimension must not be inflated into a claim in all three.
- **Learning Memory & Next Cycle:** describe continuity, revisiting, maintenance and reconstruction where supported, including when no state change exists. Replace the requirement to explain only what changed with an account of what continued and became visible.
- **Progress Tracker:** retain four authorized classification labels and their colors as scoped summaries, alongside narrative. They are not a ladder, a measurement of distance or the total learning story. Use the [Dashboard v1.1](student-dashboard-presentation-contract-v1.1.md) interpretation rule.
- **Class Reports:** use the five visible narrative functions and ten complete semantic obligations of [Class Report v1.1](architecture/CLASS-REPORT-PRESENTATION-CONTRACT-V1.1_2026-09-19.md).

Other v1.0 requirements continue, including authorized evidence, Portuguese family explanations, appropriate English examples, private teacher control tables and source-grounded next actions. No new status, inferred attendance or fabricated completeness is introduced.
