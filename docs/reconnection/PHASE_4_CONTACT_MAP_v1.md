# 6. Outreach constraints

- no mass message;
- no immediate job request;
- no immediate partnership request;
- no “look what I built” dump;
- no AI-first pitch;
- no pressure through nostalgia;
- no assumption that old affection = current professional alignment.

Preferred sequence:

```text

→ genuine catch-up
→ current context
→ mutual curiosity
→ selective PRIME reveal
→ only then possible professional overlap
```
