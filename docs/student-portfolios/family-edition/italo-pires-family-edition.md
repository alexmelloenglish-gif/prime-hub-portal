## 15. Grammar & Accuracy Overview

Current priorities are high-value grammar patterns that support clearer professional communication.

- Verb patterns: use start + verb-ing / to + verb; want + to + verb; need + to + verb in professional answers.
- Agreement & wording: use singular agreement consistently and choose precise professional wording, e.g. Each company has its own interview process.
- Time reference: use Present Simple for recurring conditions, Present Continuous for what is happening now, and will for future schedules or plans.
