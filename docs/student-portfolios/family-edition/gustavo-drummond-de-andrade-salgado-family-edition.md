## 15. Grammar & Accuracy Overview

The aim is not to collect grammar labels. These language patterns matter because they help Gustavo tell stories, understand time, give advice, describe relationships between actions and people, compare things, and use English to learn.

- Talking about past experiences: use forms such as went and drank, and didn't + base verb.
- Understanding time in questions: Do...? usually asks about routines or the present; Did...? asks about completed past events.
- Giving advice: use should / shouldn't to say what someone could do.
- Actions that return to the same person: myself, yourself, himself, herself.
- Comparing one thing with all the others: shorter words often use -est; longer words often use the most; some forms are irregular, such as best.
- Science in English: useful academic vocabulary and sequencing remain available for reuse when relevant.
