## 15. Grammar & Accuracy Overview

Rafael's current precision work is intentionally narrow: tense selection, complete and natural sentence framing, and accurate phrasing during spontaneous speech. Older observed patterns remain in cumulative memory rather than competing with the current three.

- Present perfect vs simple past: distinguish unfinished or present-relevant situations from completed past actions; keep habits separate from one-off events.
- Natural reflective framing & sentence completion: connect past experience to present meaning with complete sentences, accurate collocations and smoother transitions.
- Past tense consistency: continue consolidating completed past forms while avoiding unnecessary continuous framing in narratives.
- Natural phrasing & comparatives: avoid literal translations and choose more idiomatic English structures.
- Passive voice for reporting: use passive forms appropriately in formal and news-style communication.
- Say vs Tell: maintain the correct listener/message structure.
- Third conditional: use had + past participle and would have + past participle for unreal past outcomes.
