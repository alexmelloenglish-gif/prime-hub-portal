## 15. Grammar & Accuracy Overview

Maria Fernanda's current B2→C1 work emphasizes accurate control of advanced formal structures and transfer to independent production.

- Inversion after negative/limiting expressions: Never have I…, Rarely do I…, Under no circumstances can we….
- Auxiliary selection: distinguish do/does/did, have/has/had and modals; after did, keep the base form.
- Formal conditional inversion: recognize and produce forms such as Should you need… where appropriate.
