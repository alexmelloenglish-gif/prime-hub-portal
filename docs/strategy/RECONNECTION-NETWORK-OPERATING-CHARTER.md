# RECONNECTION NETWORK — OPERATING CHARTER

**Status:** STRATEGIC WORKING BASELINE  
**Owner:** Alexandre Mello / PRIME Digital Hub  
**Purpose:** Govern professional reconnection, collaboration and responsibility around the next phase of PRIME.

## 1. Why this exists

The Reconnection Network is not a networking campaign and not a mass outreach exercise.

It exists to rebuild a professional network around the accumulated trajectory from Cultura Inglesa → Prime Language School → physical operation → Prime Digital Hub → PRIME Learning Intelligence Engine, making the transformation legible without turning relationships into transactional acquisition.

The objective is not to collect contacts. It is to reconnect selectively with people who have history, context, credibility or strategic relevance and to determine, over time, whether there is sufficient alignment for a deeper professional relationship.

## 2. Core operating principle

> **Reconnect through shared history. Evaluate through present behavior. Deepen only through demonstrated alignment.**

No one receives strategic access, responsibility or symbolic proximity to PRIME merely because they are known, liked, enthusiastic or verbally committed.

Commitment is not a declaration. Commitment is demonstrated through consistency, follow-through, judgment and delivery over time.

## 3. PRIME principles that must remain visible

Every relationship, communication and collaboration connected to this project must remain compatible with the following principles:

1. **Student-centered design** — decisions should improve the learner's ability to understand, continue and act on their learning.
2. **Teacher authority** — technology and AI prepare, organize and support; the authorized teacher interprets and decides.
3. **Meaningful learning** — value should be felt in the experience, not hidden behind theory or technical language.
4. **Traceable evidence** — claims should be grounded in observable, attributable or teacher-validated evidence rather than speculation.
5. **Technology as infrastructure** — AI, automation and architecture are means, not the identity of the educational proposition.
6. **Sober communication** — communicate concretely, avoid inflated promises, unsupported superiority claims and unnecessary defensiveness.
7. **Continuity** — what is learned, observed and decided should remain useful beyond a single interaction.
8. **Human accountability** — important decisions must have a responsible human owner.

## 4. The three gates

### Gate 1 — RECONNECT

Purpose: restore a human and professional connection without making an immediate ask.

Sequence:
- understand the person's current situation;
- reconnect through authentic shared history;
- acknowledge the relationship before presenting any opportunity;
- avoid mass messaging or generic outreach;
- reveal PRIME selectively and only when the conversation creates a natural reason to do so.

A successful reconnection creates context, not obligation.

### Gate 2 — ALIGNMENT

Purpose: determine whether the person should move closer to the current PRIME ecosystem.

Evaluate:
- shared understanding of the problem PRIME is solving;
- respect for teacher authority;
- respect for learner-centered design;
- evidence orientation;
- communication judgment;
- reliability and follow-through;
- ability to disagree constructively;
- professional ethics;
- strategic relevance;
- competitive or reputational conflicts;
- ability to operate without creating dependency on Alexandre for basic execution.

Passing this gate does not yet mean partnership, employment, equity, commercial access or strategic authority.

### Gate 3 — COLLABORATION

Purpose: grant bounded responsibility only after alignment is demonstrated.

Before deeper collaboration, define explicitly:
- role;
- scope;
- decision rights;
- expected deliverables;
- deadlines or operating cadence;
- confidentiality requirements;
- commercial terms when applicable;
- access boundaries;
- review point;
- exit condition.

Responsibility should expand only after successful delivery at the previous level.

## 5. Relationship tiers

The network should distinguish relationship depth instead of treating every reconnection as equivalent.

### Tier A — Historical relationship
Someone worth reconnecting with because of shared history, mutual respect or continuity.

No strategic expectation is assumed.

### Tier B — Trusted professional peer
Someone with demonstrated judgment and useful reciprocal professional exchange.

May receive more visibility into PRIME, but no automatic operating authority.

### Tier C — Contributor
Someone who accepts a bounded task or workstream and demonstrates reliable execution.

Access is limited to what the role requires.

### Tier D — Strategic collaborator
Someone who has repeatedly demonstrated alignment, delivery, discretion and capacity to make good decisions without constant supervision.

This tier is earned over time.

### Tier E — Strategic partner
Reserved for relationships where incentives, reputation, contribution and long-term direction are materially aligned.

This should be rare.

## 6. What does not count as alignment

The following are positive social signals but are insufficient for strategic trust:
- enthusiasm;
- compliments;
- promises;
- shared nostalgia;
- fast agreement;
- willingness to 'help with anything';
- status, title or network alone;
- a single strong delivery;
- personal affinity without professional reliability.

The project must not confuse emotional reconnection with operating readiness.

## 7. Demonstrated commitment test

A person should be considered increasingly reliable only when observable behavior supports it.

Signals include:
- does what they said they would do;
- communicates early when something changes;
- distinguishes fact from assumption;
- protects confidential context;
- does not overclaim;
- can receive correction without becoming defensive;
- identifies risks rather than hiding them;
- makes progress without requiring continuous rescue;
- documents relevant decisions;
- respects the defined authority boundary;
- protects PRIME's reputation when acting on its behalf.

Repeated inconsistency is data. It should not be rationalized indefinitely because of history or affection.

## 8. Strategic-fit test for deeper relationships

Before considering a substantial collaboration, partnership, acquisition-like relationship, integration or commercial dependency, assess:

1. problem/product overlap;
2. pedagogical compatibility;
3. data and architecture compatibility;
4. teacher workflow fit;
5. commercial fit;
6. reputational risk;
7. competitive risk;
8. integration cost;
9. governance and authority;
10. who actively sponsors and owns the relationship on both sides.

Only after strategic, technical and commercial fit are evidenced should irreversible commitment increase.

## 9. Communication discipline

The Reconnection Network should sound like Alexandre at his best professional level: clear, warm, rigorous and concrete.

Do:
- reference real shared history;
- be specific;
- show current work through outcomes and principles;
- make invitations proportionate to the relationship;
- leave room for the other person to respond freely.

Do not:
- perform success;
- exaggerate scale;
- imply certainty where there is exploration;
- pressure someone through nostalgia;
- present every reconnection as a business opportunity;
- expose sensitive internal architecture unnecessarily;
- make claims PRIME cannot evidence.

## 10. Separation of audiences

The Reconnection Network, PRIME landing page and product architecture have different jobs.

- **Landing page:** shows the learner experience simply.
- **Reconnection Network:** shows professional trajectory, principles and selected evolution to relevant people.
- **PRIME Learning Intelligence Engine:** demonstrates deeper capability when appropriate.
- **Internal operating documents:** preserve architecture, governance and implementation detail.

Do not collapse these layers into one communication surface.

## 12. Reputation firewall

No person should represent PRIME externally, access sensitive strategic context or make commitments on its behalf without an explicit role boundary.

When representation exists, the standard is:
- no unsupported promises;
- no invented product capability;
- no disparagement of competitors;
- no disclosure beyond authorization;
- no commitment of Alexandre's time, money or reputation without authority;
- no ambiguity about what is exploratory versus operational.

## 13. Exit and de-escalation principle

A relationship can remain personally positive while becoming professionally narrower.

When alignment or reliability is insufficient:
- reduce scope;
- remove unnecessary access;
- document what remains open;
- close commitments cleanly;
- preserve dignity;
- avoid public conflict unless required for protection.

The objective is not to punish misalignment. It is to prevent misalignment from becoming structural risk.

## 14. Initial execution sequence

### Phase A — Map
Create a prioritized list beginning with people who already know Alexandre's work: former managers, former colleagues, people he supervised and selected strategic peers.

### Phase B — Context
For each person, capture only what is needed to reconnect intelligently:
- shared history;
- current role/context;
- reason the relationship matters;
- possible relevance to PRIME;
- known risks or sensitivities;
- appropriate first contact.

### Phase C — Reconnect
Send individualized messages based on history, not opportunity.

### Phase D — Observe
Record response quality, reciprocity, curiosity, reliability and natural areas of overlap.

### Phase E — Deepen selectively
Introduce Prime Digital Hub and the PRIME Learning Intelligence Engine only where useful and proportionate.

### Phase F — Pilot collaboration
Where real alignment emerges, define one bounded test before creating a broad role.

### Phase G — Review
After the test, classify the relationship: maintain, deepen, narrow or close professionally.

## 15. Success criteria

The project is working when:
- Alexandre reconnects with meaningful people without appearing transactional;
- the current PRIME trajectory becomes understandable to the right people;
- collaborations emerge from demonstrated fit rather than urgency;
- people around the project understand the principles before receiving authority;
- commitments become more reliable because scope and ownership are explicit;
- Alexandre's reputation becomes stronger as the network expands;
- professional relationships can deepen without sacrificing pedagogical or ethical standards.

## 16. Canonical sentence

> **History opens the door. Alignment builds trust. Delivery earns responsibility.**
